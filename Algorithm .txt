Algorithm: Pharmacy – Medicine Sale & Billing System

Step 1: Start the program.

Step 2: Enter the Medicine Details:
Medicine ID
Medicine Name
Price
Expiry Date
Available Stock

Step 3: Store the medicine details in the system.

Step 4: Enter the Customer Details:
Customer ID
Customer Name
Phone Number

Step 5: Select the medicine that the customer wants to purchase.

Step 6: Enter the required quantity of the medicine.

Step 7: Check whether the required quantity is available in stock.

Step 8: If the required quantity is greater than the available stock, display “Insufficient Stock” and stop the sale.

Step 9: If sufficient stock is available, calculate the medicine sale amount:
Sale Amount = Price × Quantity

Step 10: Add the sale amount to the customer's bill.

Step 11: Reduce the sold quantity from the available stock:
Updated Stock = Available Stock − Quantity Sold
Step 12: Calculate the Subtotal of all medicines purchased.

Step 13: Check whether a discount is applicable.

Step 14: If applicable, calculate and subtract the discount from the subtotal.

Step 15: Calculate GST/Tax, if applicable.

Step 16: Calculate the final bill amount:
Final Bill = Subtotal − Discount + GST/Tax

Step 17: Display the Final Bill containing:
Customer details
Medicine details
Quantity
Price
Sale amount
Subtotal
Discount
GST/Tax
Final bill amount

Step 18: Save/update the sale details and medicine stock.

Step 19: Generate the bill for the customer.

Step 20: Stop the program.
