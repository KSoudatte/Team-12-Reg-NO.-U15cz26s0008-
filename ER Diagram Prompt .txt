ER Diagram Prompt

Create a professional, academically correct, high-resolution ER (Entity–Relationship) diagram for a “Pharmacy – Medicine Sale & Billing System”.
The diagram should be designed in standard Chen ER notation and should clearly show entities, attributes, relationships, primary keys, foreign keys, and cardinalities.

1. Use these standard ER symbols
Rectangle = Entity
Oval = Attribute
Diamond = Relationship
Underlined attribute = Primary Key (PK)
(FK) = Foreign Key
1 : N = One-to-Many
1 : 1 = One-to-One
Connecting lines must connect ENTITY ↔ RELATIONSHIP ↔ ENTITY only.
Do NOT connect relationships to attributes.

3. EXACTLY 7 PROPER ENTITY RELATIONSHIPS
Relationship 1
CUSTOMER — MAKES — SALE
Cardinality: 1 : N
One customer can make many sales, while each sale belongs to one customer.
Relationship 2
SALE — GENERATES — BILL
Cardinality: 1 : 1
One sale generates one bill, and each bill belongs to one sale.
Relationship 3
SALE — CONTAINS — SALE_ITEM
Cardinality: 1 : N
One sale can contain many sale items, while each sale item belongs to one sale.
Relationship 4
MEDICINE — IS_INCLUDED_IN — SALE_ITEM
Cardinality: 1 : N
One medicine can appear in many sale items, while each sale item refers to one medicine.
Relationship 5
SUPPLIER — SUPPLIES — MEDICINE
Cardinality: 1 : N
One supplier can supply many medicines, while each medicine is supplied by one supplier.
Relationship 6
PHARMACIST — PROCESSES — SALE
Cardinality: 1 : N
One pharmacist can process many sales, while each sale is processed by one pharmacist.
Relationship 7
PHARMACIST — MANAGES — MEDICINE
Cardinality: 1 : N
One pharmacist can manage many medicine records, while each medicine record is managed by one pharmacist.

4. Recommended Layout
Arrange the diagram cleanly as follows:
Top row:
CUSTOMER → MAKES → SALE → GENERATES → BILL
Middle row:
MEDICINE → IS_INCLUDED_IN → SALE_ITEM
Bottom-left:
SUPPLIER → SUPPLIES → MEDICINE
Right/bottom:
PHARMACIST → PROCESSES → SALE
PHARMACIST → MANAGES → MEDICINE
Keep SALE and SALE_ITEM near the center because they are the main entities of the billing system.

5. Important Visual Requirements
Every relationship must connect two entities only.
Do not connect a relationship diamond directly to an attribute oval.
Do not duplicate any relationship.
Do not show “SUPPLIER — SUPPLIES — MEDICINE” twice.
Do not use SALE_ITEM — PROCESSES — PHARMACIST; the correct relationship is PHARMACIST — PROCESSES — SALE.
Clearly show all 1:N and 1:1 cardinalities beside the connecting lines.
Keep PK attributes underlined.
Clearly label FK attributes.
Keep attribute ovals close to their corresponding entity.
Avoid crossing or overlapping lines.
Use straight, clean connector lines.
Use a white background and professional academic colors.
Make all text large, sharp, and readable.
Include a small Legend explaining Entity, Attribute, Relationship, PK, FK, 1:N and 1:1.
Add the title “Pharmacy – Medicine Sale & Billing System” and subtitle “ER Diagram” at the top.
